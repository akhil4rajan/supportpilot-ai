# Appointments and outpatient clinics

## Booking an appointment
Outpatient appointments need a referral from your GP or specialist. Our booking team can be reached on (02) 5550 0110 between 8:00am and 5:00pm, Monday to Friday. Please have your referral and Medicare card ready.

## Changing or cancelling
Please give at least 24 hours notice to reschedule or cancel so that another patient can use the time. Changes are made by the booking team, not by this assistant, and I can connect you with them.

## Arriving for your appointment
Please arrive 15 minutes early to check in at the clinic reception on the level shown on your appointment. First-time patients should allow extra time to complete registration. Bring your Medicare card, referral and any recent imaging or test results.

## Pathology collection
Blood tests are collected from the Ground Floor Collection Centre between 7:00am and 4:00pm on weekdays and 7:00am to 11:00am on Saturdays. Some tests need fasting. Your referral or request form will say if yours does.

## Interpreter services
Free interpreter services are available in over 100 languages. Let the booking team know when you book, or ask at reception.
