# Parking and getting here

## Address
Wattle Bay Hospital is at 120 Harbour Road, Wattle Bay NSW. The main entrance is on Harbour Road, and the emergency entrance is on Banksia Lane.

## Parking and parking costs
Parking is in the public multi-level car park under the main building, with entry from Banksia Lane. The car park is open 24 hours. Parking is free for the first 30 minutes and the daily maximum parking cost is $28. Pay for parking by card or the parking app at the pay stations or the exit. Disability parking is on Level 1 near the lifts, and a valid permit must be displayed.

## Discounted weekly passes
Frequent visitors, such as parents of children in hospital and carers of long-stay patients, can apply for a discounted weekly pass at the main reception.

## Public transport
Wattle Bay Hospital is a 5-minute walk from the Wattle Bay ferry wharf and the bus stop on Harbour Road, with regular services to the city. Use your Opal card or contactless payment.

## Drop-off
A short-stay drop-off zone is available at the main entrance for up to 10 minutes.
