# Emergency and urgent care

## Emergency department
For a life-threatening emergency, call 000 for an ambulance. The Wattle Bay Hospital emergency department is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and patients are seen in order of clinical need rather than arrival time, so a person with a more serious condition may be seen before you.

## Urgent care clinic
The urgent care clinic treats minor injuries and illnesses such as sprains, small cuts, ear infections and minor burns. It is open from 8:00am to 10:00pm daily. No booking is needed. If your condition is serious, you will be moved to the emergency department.

## What to bring
Please bring your Medicare card, a list of your current medications, any recent referral letters, and a support person if you can. Overseas visitors should bring their passport and travel insurance details.

## Not sure where to go?
This assistant cannot give medical advice. If you are unsure whether your condition is an emergency, call 000 or speak with your GP.
