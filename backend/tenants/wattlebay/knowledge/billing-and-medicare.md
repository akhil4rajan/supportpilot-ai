# Billing and Medicare

## Public patients
Public patients who show a valid Medicare card are treated at no cost in our public hospital services. Some services, such as private rooms or non-urgent treatment requested outside the public system, may have fees.

## Private patients
If you are admitted as a private patient, your private health fund covers an agreed part of your costs. You may receive separate accounts from the hospital, your doctors, anaesthetist and pathology or radiology providers, and you may have an out-of-pocket gap payment. Contact your health fund before admission to confirm your cover.

## Overseas visitors
Visitors from countries without a reciprocal health care agreement with Australia are treated as private patients and will be asked to pay for their care. Please bring your passport and travel insurance details.

## Paying an account
You can pay an account online, by phone on (02) 5550 0120 or in person at the cashier on the Ground Floor, 8:30am to 4:30pm on weekdays. If you are having trouble paying, ask about a payment plan or financial hardship assistance, which are decided by the patient accounts team.

## Questions about a bill
Billing disputes and refunds are handled by the patient accounts team, not by this assistant. I can connect you with them.
