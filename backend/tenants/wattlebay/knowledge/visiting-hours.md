# Visiting hours

## General wards
Visiting hours for general wards are 10:00am to 8:00pm every day. We ask that visitors keep to two at a time per patient so that rest and care are not disrupted.

## Maternity
Partners and support people may visit maternity from 8:00am to 9:00pm. Other visitors are welcome from 2:00pm to 8:00pm. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times.

## Intensive care and high dependency
The intensive care unit is open to immediate family from 11:00am to 8:00pm, but please call the unit on (02) 5550 0142 before you travel as visiting may be limited when a patient is being treated. Visitors must be approved by the patient or their nominated contact.

## If you are unwell
Please do not visit if you have a fever, cough, vomiting or diarrhoea, or have been in contact with an infectious illness in the last few days. Hand sanitiser is available at every ward entrance.
