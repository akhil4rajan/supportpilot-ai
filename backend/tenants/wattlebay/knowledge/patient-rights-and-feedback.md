# Patient rights, privacy and feedback

## Your privacy
Wattle Bay Hospital keeps your health information confidential and shares it only as permitted by law or with your consent. You can ask to see or correct your health record by contacting Health Information Services on (02) 5550 0130. This assistant only ever shows you your own appointments, after you sign in.

## Making a complaint or giving feedback
We welcome feedback. You can speak to the nurse unit manager, ask for the Patient Liaison Officer, or email feedback through the hospital website. Complaints are handled by our patient liaison team and you will receive a response. Raising a concern will never affect the care you receive.

## Support for carers and families
Social workers, chaplains and interpreters are available. Ask any staff member or call main reception on (02) 5550 0100.
