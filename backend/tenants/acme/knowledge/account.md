# Account and payments

## Resetting your password
Select "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your email. We send a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive.

## Changing your email or address
Open Account > Profile to update your email, phone number and saved addresses. Address changes do not affect orders that have already shipped.

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. Gift cards can be combined with one other payment method.

## Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled while the status is "Processing". Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery.
