# Warranty and product care

## Warranty
All Acme products carry a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Normal wear and tear, misuse and accidental damage are not covered.

## Making a warranty claim
Contact support with your order number and photos of the defect. Claims are reviewed by a human agent within 2 business days.

## Washing the Trail Runner Jacket
Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle with a technical-fabric detergent. Do not use fabric softener. Tumble dry low or hang to dry.
