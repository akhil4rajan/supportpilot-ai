# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3-7 business days within the continental US. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and is available for orders placed before 2pm ET. Orders to Alaska, Hawaii and US territories take 5-10 business days.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on orders over $75; otherwise it costs $6.95. Express shipping costs $19.95 on every order.

## Tracking your order
Once your order ships you receive an email with a tracking number. Signed-in customers can also ask this assistant for their order status at any time.

## International shipping
We currently ship to Canada and the United Kingdom. International orders take 7-14 business days and customers are responsible for customs duties and taxes.
